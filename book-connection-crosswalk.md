# Book ↔ Program crosswalk — what from *Your Past Loves You* plugs in where

**Purpose:** the concrete answer to "what can I include from the book, and how does it connect."
Maps each chapter of the book to where its *ideas* feed the VGM research and the VG Lyceum courses.

**The one rule (from the book's own contract):** the flow is **outward only**. The research page and
the courses may *draw on the book's ideas*; we do **not** inject product talk or AI-hype back into the
book (AI stays a mirror there), and we **never** spoil the Ch 13 reveal. Same discipline as the papers.

## The crosswalk

| Book chapter | Core idea (plain) | Plugs into |
|---|---|---|
| **Ch 1** — What is a thing, really? (`connection`, `relational-ontology`) | A thing is defined by its relationships. | **VGM premise** — this *is* the "why." Already cited in P3 §5; use as the `/research` page epigraph. |
| **Ch 2** — More than its parts (`emergence`, `the-ladder`) | Wholes get properties their parts don't have. | **Lyceum Track 1** (small models composed into one voice) + VGM's "small parts, composed." |
| **Ch 3** — Where is a memory? (`memory-as-relationship`, `engram`, `connectome`) | A memory isn't a place; it's a pattern of connections. | **★ Strongest hit.** The plain-English opener for **Lyceum Track 2 (memory & retrieval)** and for **connection-memory** in VGM. |
| **Ch 4** — What is a thought made of? (`distributed-representation`, `pattern-over-part`) | No single neuron thinks; the pattern does. | **AGM** (behavior-level, not weight-level) + Track 2 AI fundamentals. |
| **Ch 5** — Why do we forget? (`forgetting-as-compression`) | Forgetting is distillation, not loss. | **Track 2** memory lesson on compression / summarization / what to keep. |
| **Ch 6** — What is intelligence? (`intelligence-as-emergence`) | Knowing-how lives in connection, not one fact. | The AI-mirror bridge; VGM "model-free where deterministic suffices." |
| **Ch 7** — Can a machine remember? (`persistent-memory`) | What changes when a mind keeps a past. | **★ Track 2** — the literal reason a memory system exists. |
| **Ch 8** — Human/machine line? (`substrate-independence`) | Mind is a pattern; the material is secondary. | **VGM local-first / sovereign** (it runs on *your* hardware). |
| **Ch 9** — Why does time move one way? (`arrow-of-time`, `entropy`) | Memory is the record of order over time. | **★ AGM / Metron** — the loveliest way to explain *why Metron watches over time* (drift & recurrence). |
| **Ch 10** — Why does grief hurt? (`love-as-preserved-connection`, `grief-as-broken-edge`) | Love is a kept connection; grief an edge to a gone node. | **Book-only.** The soul of the book — do not strip-mine for courses. |
| **Ch 11** — Who were you? (`network-identity`, `past-selves-persist`) | Identity is an accumulating network, not a fixed point. | **Connection-memory model of identity** + AGM's carried mood; Track 2. |
| **Ch 12** — What is consciousness? (`consciousness-as-emergence`) | Awareness as emergent from interconnected memory in time. | **AGM** as "a subconscious made legible"; the self-model theme. |
| **Ch 13** — Does your past love you? (the reveal) | The payoff. | **Book-only. Never spoil.** This is why the book exists. |

## What to actually include (3 concrete moves)

1. **`/research` page + P3 §5 epigraph.** One or two non-spoiler lines from **Ch 1** as the humanistic
   framing of the whole program. (P3 already names the book; this makes the tie visible on the site.)
2. **Lyceum Track 2 "read this first" companions.** **Ch 3, 5, 7, 11** are the plain-English foundation
   for the memory & retrieval track. Adapt their *ideas* into short lesson intros — the book gives Track 2
   a human on-ramp competitors don't have. (Adaptations live in the course; the book stays literary.)
3. **AGM/Metron explainer.** Borrow **Ch 9's** time/entropy framing to explain, for a general reader,
   why a self-model needs an organ that watches over time. Great for the announcement post / a Lyceum lesson.

## The shape of it

- **Book = the why, for humans** (Part I + the emotional spine: Ch 1, 10, 13 carry the heart).
- **Part II + Ch 7, 11 = the memory curriculum's soul** → Lyceum Track 2 + VGM connection-memory.
- **Ch 9, 12, 4 = the self-model thread** → AGM.
- **Ch 8 = sovereignty** → VGM local-first.
- Everything routes back to **Ch 1's `connection`** — which is also the spine of VGM. Same idea, two registers.
