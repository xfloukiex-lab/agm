## Fund independent AI research that shows its work

**We build AI systems that run on your own hardware, learn without training, and publish every
result — including the ones that failed.**

Vektorgeist is a small independent research shop. In the last month we published four papers
(open access, permanent DOIs) building one idea: much of what AI does with billions of trained
weights can be done with **no trainable weights at all** — deterministic geometry plus an
append-only memory. Measured, on real data:

- **Learning that is instant.** Add five examples of a new class and the system recognizes it
  immediately. No training run. No GPU.
- **Forgetting that is impossible.** Taught classes in sequence, a standard network loses 50–80%
  of what it learned first. Ours loses exactly nothing — provably, by construction.
- **5–10× less data** to reach a trained network's accuracy, and next-step prediction that beats
  a trained network 3–16× with zero learned parameters.

And the part we're proudest of: **the public study record.** Every finding is logged — currently
including 6 negative results and 6 retractions kept visible with their original wording. When a
result of ours dies under a control, that stays public too. Research you can audit beats research
you have to trust.

**Where the money goes:** hardware for the experiments, publication costs, and time — the papers
and tools are free and open (CC-BY-4.0 / Apache-2.0) and stay that way.

- The papers: vektorgeist.com/research
- The living study record: xfloukiex-lab.github.io/hodos-study
- The tools we've shipped open: github.com/xfloukiex-lab
