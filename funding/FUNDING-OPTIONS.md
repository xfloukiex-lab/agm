## What we are funding

The VektorGeist research program: model-free / locally-sovereign AI (the published paper series

## Donation-lane platforms (small money, low friction)

| Platform | Fit for research | Fees | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GitHub Sponsors** | STRONG — funds open-source dev/research directly | 0% platform fee | Ties to the org (`xfloukiex-lab`) where the public work already lives; recurring tiers; lowest-trust-barrier for a technical audience; payout via Stripe (identity = owner's, not public) |
| **Open Collective** | STRONG — made for open projects; transparent budget page | ~10% all-in via fiscal host | The transparency ledger is itself good optics for a research record built on honesty |
| **Ko-fi** | OK — simple one-off + monthly | 0–5% | Lighter-weight, less credibility signal for research |
| **GoFundMe** | WEAK fit — geared to personal/charity causes | ~2.9%+$0.30 | Research/startup asks underperform there; discoverability is life-events-oriented; still possible, but the audience mismatch is real |
| Patreon | OK — creator-content framing | 8–12% | Fits if the Lyceum videos become the funded product; not the papers |

**Recommendation: GitHub Sponsors first** (0%, technical audience, sits on the org that already
hosts hodos-study/pallium/magpie-search), **Open Collective second** if a transparent budget page
is wanted. GoFundMe only if the goal is reaching a non-technical audience — and then the copy
must be written for laypeople, not researchers.

## Investor lane (different animal — not a donation page)

- Investors buy equity/SAFEs in **Vektorgeist (the company)**, not in papers. That needs: a
  one-pager, a deck, a data room (products: warden, magpie-search, Aviary, Mnema; the research
  moat), and a decision about how much of the company is for sale — ALL owner+Lee decisions
  (co-owned company).
- Anything securities-shaped (soliciting investment publicly) has legal exposure that a donation
  page does not. General solicitation rules differ by jurisdiction and vehicle. The safe form:
  a private one-pager sent to specific people the owners choose, not a public "invest in us" page.
- Realistic first rungs before VC-shaped money: angel checks from people already in the orbit,
  revenue from the shipped products, grant stack (NLnet decision pending; other open calls exist).
